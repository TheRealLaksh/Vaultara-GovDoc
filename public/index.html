What keeps your documents private.

Private by default

An uploaded document can be read only by the account that owns it, until the owner shares it.

Sharing needs a verified email

People you share with, and members of your family group, only see documents after they verify their email address.

Family IDs work like passwords

A Family ID is 16 random characters. Anyone who has it can see documents tagged to it, so share it only with people you trust.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

Vaultara runs on Google Firebase, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. Access rules are enforced on the server, not just in the page.

Vaultara is a student project. It is not affiliated with any government body and does not replace official services such as DigiLocker.
